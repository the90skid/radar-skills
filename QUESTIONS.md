# Questions to ask your AI

Radar gives your AI the nightly record of what your competitors run, what they push, what they kill and where they send people. These questions get the most out of it. Swap in your own brands.

## Start here

1. "Set me up on Radar. My site is mybrand.com."
2. "I compete in the United Kingdom. Track my competitors there."
3. "What are my competitors doing right now?"
4. "What changed with my competitors this week?"
5. "What should I test next?"

## See what is working for them

6. "What are Grüns' highest performers right now, and what do they have in common?"
7. "Which of Carpe's ads have run the longest, and what do they have in common?"
8. "What is Javvy pushing hard that is less than two weeks old?"
9. "Which creator videos is Blissy running from partner pages, and how do they rank against its own?"
10. "Show me the ad PetLab is pushing hardest and break down its hook, script and landing page."

## Angles

11. "What are the main angles my competitors are pushing?"
12. "Which angle do all three of my competitors share, and which angle does only one of them run?"
13. "What angles has Grüns dropped in the last two months?"
14. "Which words and phrases does Grüns use most in its top ads?"
15. "Group Carpe's top 100 ads by the problem they open with."

## Hooks

16. "What hooks open my competitors' best ads?"
17. "Give me fill-in hook templates from the hooks they have kept running longest."
18. "Which hook types appear in my category but not in their ads?"
19. "What new hooks did they launch in the last two weeks?"
20. "Find ads in my category that open with a warning."

## What they killed

21. "What did Ultra Pouches try and kill this month?"
22. "How long does Javvy give a new ad before switching it off?"
23. "Which long-running ad did they just end, and what replaced it?"
24. "What do their fastest-killed ads have in common?"

## Landing pages

25. "Where does Grüns' top ad send people? Break that page down section by section."
26. "What changed on that page in the last month?"
27. "Is any competitor A/B testing a landing page right now, and what is the difference between the versions?"
28. "Which kind of page do my competitors' best ads go to: product page, advertorial, listicle or quiz?"
29. "Put their top ad next to its landing page. Which lines from the ad appear on the page?"

## Scripts

30. "Break down Carpe's top video beat by beat and give me a template."
31. "Take their three best videos and tell me the structure they share."
32. "Turn that template into a script for my product."
33. "Write a creator brief from that ad that my UGC creator can shoot tomorrow."

## Your whole category

34. "What is working across supplements right now?"
35. "Which hooks and page types do most high performers in my category share?"
36. "Which formats are rising in skincare this month?"
37. "What page type do the best pet ads send to?"

## Decide and make

38. "Give me five tests for next week, ranked, each with the competitor ads that back it."
39. "I can make three videos and two statics a week. What should they be?"
40. "Write three ads for my product on three different angles my competitors have proven."

## Keep it going

41. "Every Monday, tell me what changed with my competitors."
42. "We tested the GLP-1 angle and it beat our control. Save that."
43. "What did we learn last month, and what should we test now given that?"
44. "Add Seed to my competitors and run the overview again."
45. "Track Halara in the United Kingdom and show me what it is pushing hardest there."

## Go deeper on one answer

46. "Show me that ad."
47. "Show me the landing page."
48. "Which three ads back that up? Give me their rank, share and days live."
49. "Which of those ads ranks highest and has run longest? Adapt its structure for my brand."
50. "Give me the links to every ad you mentioned."

## Chains that work well

- **Monday in ten minutes:** 4 → 23 → 38
- **Planning a shoot:** 11 → 16 → 30 → 32 → 33
- **New competitor appeared:** 44 → 6 → 25 → 21
- **Refreshing a tired account:** 11 → 14 → 40
