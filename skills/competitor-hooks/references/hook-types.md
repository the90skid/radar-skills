# Hook types

Give each opening line one type. Pick the one that describes what the first sentence does to the viewer.

| Type | What the first line does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Problem named | States a pain in the viewer's words | "Can't poop on a GLP-1." |
| Question | Asks something the viewer wants answered | "GLP-1 side effects?" |
| Confession | A person admits something | "I was embarrassed to take my shirt off." |
| Contrarian | Says the common belief is wrong | "Stop buying probiotics." |
| Warning | Tells the viewer not to do something | "Do not buy this on Amazon." |
| Callout | Names who the ad is for | "If you're on a weight loss journey, watch this." |
| Result first | Opens on the outcome | "Three weeks in and my bloating is gone." |
| Story start | Begins a personal story | "So my doctor told me something last month." |
| Demonstration | Shows or tells what is about to be shown | "Watch what happens when I pour this out." |
| Comparison | Sets two things side by side | "This versus the one you get at the pharmacy." |
| Social proof | Opens on numbers or popularity | "There's a reason this sold out four times." |
| Authority | An expert speaks | "As a dermatologist, this is what I use." |
| Curiosity gap | Promises something without saying what | "Nobody tells you this about collagen." |
| Offer | Leads with the deal | "You get the starter kit free today." |
| Shown, no speech | The video has no spoken line | (describe from the headline) |

## Turning a hook into a template

1. Keep the structure and the rhythm.
2. Replace the product, the problem and the detail with slots in square brackets.
3. Keep one concrete slot. "Until [specific moment]" beats "until [something happened]".
4. Drop the competitor's claim, number and name.

| Hook | Template |
|---|---|
| "Can't poop on a GLP-1." | "Can't [basic thing] on [new situation]." |
| "Do not buy this on Amazon." | "Do not buy [product] on [usual place]." |
| "Nobody tells you this about collagen." | "Nobody tells you this about [category]." |
| "I thought greens powders tasted like grass." | "I thought [category] all [bad thing]." |

## Reading a hook list

- One hook on many creatives: the brand runs one opening line over several different ads. Give the count.
- Many hooks on one body: the brand runs several openings over the same ad. Give the count.
- A type that appears only on killed ads was tried and dropped.
